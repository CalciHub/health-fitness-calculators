# Health & Fitness Calculators

Health and fitness calculators from [CalciHub](https://calcihub.com/) for estimating common health, nutrition, fitness, activity, and body-composition measures.

This repository documents the health and fitness calculator collection available on CalciHub and provides references to the individual calculators and their calculation methods.

## What this repository contains

The Health & Fitness collection currently includes **18 calculators** covering areas such as:

- Body composition
- Calories and energy expenditure
- Weight and nutrition
- Activity and exercise
- Sleep and daily habits
- Reproductive health estimates
- Other health-related calculations

See the [Health & Fitness Calculator Index](calculators/README.md) for the complete list.

## Important note

Health and fitness calculators provide estimates based on the information entered and the formulas or assumptions used.

They are intended for informational and educational purposes and should not be treated as medical diagnosis or professional medical advice.

## Calculation methodology

Where relevant, calculator documentation explains:

- The formula or method used
- What each input represents
- Assumptions behind the estimate
- How the result should be interpreted
- Relevant limitations
